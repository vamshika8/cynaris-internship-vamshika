# Week 3 Day 5 — KPI Dashboard Narrative

## 1. Introduction

The Retail KPI Dashboard was created to analyse sales performance using key metrics and visualisations. It helps understand revenue, product sales, regional distribution, and sales activity over time.

## 2. Key Findings

- **Total Sales Revenue:** The total recorded revenue is ₹1,62,000.
- **Units Sold:** A total of 8 units were sold.
- **Distinct Products:** The dataset contains three products: Laptop, Phone, and Tablet.
- **Sales by Product:** Laptop generated the highest revenue at ₹85,000, followed by Phone at ₹45,000 and Tablet at ₹32,000.
- **Sales by Region:** All recorded sales belong to the North region, accounting for 100% of the available sales.
- **Sales Trend:** The dashboard displays sales across the recorded transaction dates, but more data is needed to identify reliable trends.

## 3. Observations

Laptop contributes the largest share of recorded revenue, while Phone has the highest sales quantity, with four units sold. These findings provide a basic overview of product performance.

However, the dataset contains only three transactions from one region. Profit and margin values are also unavailable, so profitability and broader business performance cannot be evaluated reliably.

## 4. Recommendations

1. Collect more sales records across different regions and time periods.
2. Monitor product-wise revenue and units sold regularly.
3. Add cost and profit information to analyse profitability.
4. Use a larger dataset to identify meaningful sales trends.
5. Review KPI performance against business targets regularly.

## 5. Conclusion

The dashboard provides a simple overview of retail sales performance. The available data shows that Laptop generated the highest revenue, while Phone recorded the highest quantity sold. Although the dashboard demonstrates how KPIs can support business analysis, additional and more complete data is required for reliable business decisions.
