# Executive Summary: Indian E-Commerce Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses a synthetic Indian e-commerce dataset containing 100,000 orders and customer, product, and shipment information. The objective is to identify patterns in customer spending, product profitability, marketing performance, and delivery operations.

## Key Findings

1. **Customer segmentation:** The dataset contains 22,820 unique customers who placed orders. Regular customers form the largest segment (11,002), followed by Budget customers (7,713), New customers (3,294), and Premium customers (2,991).

2. **Premium customer value:** Premium customers have an average recorded total spend of ₹286,622.67. This segment may offer opportunities for targeted retention and loyalty initiatives.

3. **Delivery performance:** Average delivery time across the available shipment records is 4.10 days. Same-Day shipping averages 0.83 days, Next-Day averages 1.81 days, and Standard shipping averages 4.81 days. Delivery performance should be assessed alongside each shipping method's delay rate and service expectations.

## Actionable Recommendations

1. **Strengthen premium-customer retention:** Test personalised offers, loyalty rewards, and early access to promotions for Premium customers. Evaluate repeat purchases and customer spending before and after the campaign.

2. **Improve delivery reliability:** Investigate delayed shipments by shipping method and identify operational bottlenecks. Track average delivery time and delay rate regularly to evaluate improvements.

3. **Optimise marketing investment:** Compare marketing channels using order value, order count, and customer acquisition results. Prioritise channels only after considering campaign costs and conversion performance, not order value alone.

## Limitations

The dataset is synthetic, so findings represent patterns in the provided data rather than verified real-world business performance. Order value should not automatically be interpreted as realised revenue, and marketing profitability cannot be determined without campaign cost data.

## Tools Used

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Jupyter Notebook.
